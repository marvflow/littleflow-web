How an Experience Morning runs

Two hours of an ordinary morning.

We don’t put on a special programme for you. You arrive in the middle of what the children do every Thursday.

8:30
👋

Welcome and morning circle

Your child joins the morning circle with the others — in Czech and English. You stay close by for as long as it’s needed.

9:00
🔭

Project block

The main part of the morning. You see project-based learning in action — how children ask, create and work together.

10:00
☕

Time for your questions

While the children are busy, you have time with the teachers and the leadership. Ask anything — adaptation, language, fees, places.

10:30
🌳

Goodbye

The children head outside and you leave with a real picture — not a brochure.

Each Experience Morning has limited capacity so it stays calm. Please book ahead.

People at Little FLOW

Who you’ll meet.

A small group — no more than 12 children. We have the space to really get to know each child, their pace, needs and interests.

You’ll meet them in person at an Experience Morning — that’s exactly why we run it.

How to apply

Admission in three steps.

A simple process, no bureaucracy. We want to get to know you — not bury you in forms.

01

We get to know each other

An Experience Morning with your child, or Coffee with Management. You see the kindergarten running normally and ask whatever you need.

Pick a time →
02

We agree on the start

We go through the programme option, the start date and an individual adaptation plan so the beginning is as smooth as possible.

03

Sign the contract

We confirm the terms and the place. Welcome to Little FLOW.

Joining without stress

Adaptation tailored to every child.

There’s no fixed schedule — we adapt to the child, not the other way around. Parents are part of the process from day one.

We start before day one

Meetings, events and visits to the kindergarten before the first day — the child arrives to a familiar environment.

3–4 weeks of adaptation

Adaptation typically takes 3–4 weeks, but without pressure. Every child has their own pace.

Close communication

Twigsee, photos, messages from the teacher throughout the entire adaptation.
