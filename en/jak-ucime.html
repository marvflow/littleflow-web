Our approach

What Little FLOW does differently.

Six principles that shape everyday life at the kindergarten.

01

Project-based learning in practice

Children learn through themes and projects that connect several areas at once — language, movement, making, logical thinking and working together. A theme like “Our city” can lead from a walk around the neighbourhood through building a model to a shared presentation for parents.

02

Czech and English every day

Czech and English are a natural part of every day. Children meet English in play, songs, ordinary conversation and shared activities.

03

Technology without screens

We introduce technology the tangible way — by building, exploring and playing, not through a screen. No tablets, no passive consumption.

04

A small group and individual attention

Thanks to the small group we have the space to really get to know each child, to follow their pace, needs and interests, and to give them enough attention.

05

A natural path to FLOW Elementary

Little FLOW follows naturally from the environment and philosophy of FLOW Elementary. Joint events, school visits and familiar surroundings can make the move into first grade easier. How we connect kindergarten and school →

06

Partnership with parents and tracking progress

We see parents as partners. We follow each child’s development, capture their progress in a portfolio and share regularly what they are moving forward in and what we want to focus on next — through the Twigsee app and in person.

Czech and English

Two languages, one childhood.

English at Little FLOW is not a subject or an after-school club. It is a natural part of the day and children meet it in situations that make sense.

How it works

Language belongs to the moment

English appears where it makes sense — in play, a shared project, making things, movement or ordinary conversation. The child doesn’t experience the language as “learning”, but as a natural part of the day.

No pressure

Understanding comes before speaking

Nobody is pushed to answer in English. Children understand first, then start on their own. A silent period is a normal part of the process, not a problem.

Who it fits

For Czech and international children

Little FLOW creates an environment where Czech and English meet naturally. Czech children get regular contact with English, and children from international families can gradually get to know Czech in everyday situations. We use both languages naturally, in context and with respect for each child’s pace.

Project example

Our city in three steps.

This is what a project looks like in practice — from a question, through creation, to an exhibition for parents.

Step 1 · Explore

A walk to the bakery

Children get to know their neighbourhood first-hand. Questions come naturally — and with them, the project’s theme.

Step 2 · Create

Building a neighbourhood model

What the children saw becomes a model of the neighbourhood. Movement, creation, discussion and collaboration — exactly what project-based learning means.

Step 3 · Share

Mini exhibition for parents

Children guide their parents through the model and show what they made. The project ends with sharing — and another chapter in the child’s portfolio.
