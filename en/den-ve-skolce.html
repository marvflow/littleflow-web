Safety and wellbeing

A calm child learns by themselves.

A small group isn’t just a number on a website. It’s the condition for a child to feel safe — and only then to have the capacity to try new things.

Room for every emotion

Anger, sadness and tiredness belong to the day. We don’t send children off “to calm down" — we help them name the emotion and move through it.

A predictable rhythm

The same rituals every day. A child knows what comes next — and that frees their head for exploring, creating and relationships.

Every child at their own pace

We don’t compare children with each other. We follow how far each one has moved compared to themselves.

Our spaces

Light, wood and calm.

The kindergarten is located at the FLOW campus in Balabenka — together with the elementary school, with its own separate operation for the youngest.

You’ll see them best in person — every Thursday at an Experience Morning.
