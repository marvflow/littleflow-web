Following progress

Every child develops at their own pace.

We follow children’s progress continuously, record it and plan further support accordingly. It isn’t about comparing children with each other, but about understanding what a child already manages, what interests them and where they can move next.

How we do it

Three things we hold together.

Pedagogical assessment

We follow each child’s development in communication, thinking, motor skills, emotions, relationships and independence.

The child’s portfolio

Every child builds their own portfolio, capturing their work, projects, photographs, experiences and the things they are proud of.

Sharing with parents

We share regularly with parents what a child is moving forward in and what we want to focus on together next.

A pre-schooler at Little FLOW

The last year before school isn’t just worksheets.

We build the final year before school on independence, curiosity, concentration, communication and the gradual development of the skills that matter for starting school.
