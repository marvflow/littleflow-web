Common questions

Honest answers. If something is missing here, write or call us — we are happy to talk it through.

Do you take two-year-olds who are not yet out of nappies?

Yes. We take children who still wear nappies and we help them through the transition at their own pace, together with you.

Do all children have to sleep after lunch?

No. We have a sleeping area and a quiet non-sleeping area, so each child gets what they need. Those who sleep rest after lunch until about 1 p.m.; the others have quiet activities.

Who provides the meals?

Meals are delivered by our catering supplier and there is always a choice. Meals are included in the fee for every attendance option, the shorter day included.

Where do the children go outside?

Every day to the Balabenka playground — a 3-minute walk from the building. The building also has its own outdoor terrace, which we use for play and activities.

Who teaches at Little FLOW?

Qualified teachers with classroom experience, working closely with the FLOW Elementary team — so the children already know the people and the place before they move up to Year 1.

Can we come fewer than five days a week?

Yes. You choose 3, 4 or 5 days a week, and a full day or a shorter day. The combination is up to you.
